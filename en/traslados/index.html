Transfers

From the airport to your stay, no waiting, no surprises

Private round-trip transfers from Punta Cana airport (PUJ), in the vehicle that best fits your group.

Get a transfer quote

At a glance

From Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) it is about a 15-minute drive to Cap Cana, 25 to 30 minutes to Bávaro and Los Corales, 45 minutes to Uvero Alto, 1 h 15 min to Bayahíbe and La Romana and 2 h 30 min to Santo Domingo. Our transfers are private, round trip and available at any time, by sedan, SUV, van, minibus or coach depending on your group, with the price confirmed before you pay.

  • Private

    Just you and your group, no stops at other hotels.

  • Round trip

    We coordinate arrival and departure in one booking.

  • Any time

    Early-morning or late-night flights: we'll be there.

  • Driver on request

    Also for shopping, beach or dinner days.
